# Retries with exponential backoff, jitter and a retry budget — explained

## The intuition
Many failures are blips (a 503 during a deploy); retrying hides them. But every retry is extra load on a struggling service, so retries need three brakes: retry only transient errors, wait longer each time with a random spread (backoff + jitter), and stop when too many calls fail (a budget).

**Analogy:** calling back a busy shop: wait longer each time, at a random moment, and stop when your phone credit runs low.
Where the analogy breaks: a server may never answer at all, so retries also need timeouts.

```
get_with_retry ─▶ B ── 503 ─▶ is_retryable? ─no─▶ give up (404)
                               │yes
                     budget.can_retry()? ─no─▶ give up (budget empty)
                               │yes
             sleep uniform(0, min(0.5, 0.02·2^attempt)) ─▶ try again (max 4)
```

The one thing to remember: retries are load — bound them and spread them with jitter.

## Walkthrough: what actually happens when you run it
1. **You run** `python retries.py`
   **What happens inside:** `FlakyB` returns seeded 503s. A failure costs one token, a success adds 0.5; `can_retry()` needs ≥ 5 of 10.
   **What you see:**
   ```
   full-jitter ceilings (s): [0.02, 0.04, 0.08, 0.16, 0.32, 0.5]
   GET /missing -> ('gave up (404)', 1) (404: not retried)
   --- B flaky (30% 503) ---
   req 02: 200                          attempts=4 sleeps=[0.006, 0.006, 0.052] tokens=7.5
   …
   8/10 succeeded, 17 attempts (load x1.7, worst case x4.0)
   --- B sick (90% 503) ---
   …
   1/10 succeeded, 10 attempts (load x1.0, worst case x4.0)
   ```
   **What it means:** req 02 lost three tokens and gained 0.5 (10 → 7.5); each sleep sits below its ceiling. When B is sick, the budget holds load at ×1.0, not ×4.0. Matches the README.

2. **You run** `python try_it.py`
   **What you see:**
   ```
   tryIt 1: max_attempts=1: 5/10 succeeded, 10 attempts
   tryIt 1: max_attempts=4: 8/10 succeeded, 17 attempts
   tryIt 2: no jitter  : busiest 10 ms slot has 100 of 100 retries
   tryIt 2: full jitter: busiest 10 ms slot has 17 of 100 retries
   ```
   **What it means:** retries rescued 3 requests for 7 extra calls; without jitter, everyone retries at once.

3. **You run** `python agent_assist.py --fake`
   **What you see:**
   ```
   p50_ms        32.7     24.1  WRONG
   p99_ms       210.6    141.0  WRONG
   ```
   **What it means:** the fake model reported mean and max as percentiles; code recomputed and flagged them.

## Common confusions
**Q:** Retry every error. — **A:** `/missing` stops after 1 attempt: a 404 will not fix itself.

**Q:** Exponential backoff alone prevents storms. — **A:** Identical sleeps put 100 retries in one slot; jitter spreads them.

**Q:** Budgets only matter in long outages. — **A:** It tripped in phase 1 (req 07).
